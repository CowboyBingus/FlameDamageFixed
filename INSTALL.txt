Flame Damage Fixed v1.1
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer and import this ZIP into Arsenal or
HD2MM. Enable and deploy both, then restart Helldivers 2. Keep the shared
loader as the winning Wwise startup replacement.

Vanilla Plus Megapack v34 also contains this addon as an option; use either
the Megapack option or this package, not both.

What it fixes, for the Lumberer's flamethrower arm and the Flame Sentry (they
share one flame):
- Two of the flame's five damaging parts never spawned. They now spawn with
  the Cremator's timing, about 0.4 and 0.6 s into a burst. They hit but are
  not drawn, so the flame still looks like one stream.
- The flame starts at the Cremator's distances from the nozzle (1-4 m instead
  of 7-15 m), so bugs close to the Lumberer are hit too.
- The flame no longer hits the weapon that fires it, and still hits everything
  else, Chargers and other armoured targets included (v1.0's flame passed
  through them).
Damage, fuel, heat and burning values are unchanged, and other flamethrowers
are not affected.
